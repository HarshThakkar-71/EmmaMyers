Chapter One

The girl from Orlando

Before Nevermore Academy, before solving murders and surviving the Overworld — there was a homeschooled kid with a camera-facing grin and a serious plan.

Biography

Emma Elizabeth Myers

Born on April 2, 2002, in Orlando, Florida, Emma grew up in a close, creative household — with a sister, Isabel, who also caught the acting bug. Homeschooled from an early age, she had the kind of schedule that made auditions possible and the kind of focus that made them count.

She began working professionally as a child, landing her first television role around 2010. For the next decade she quietly stacked up experience — holiday movies, indie dramas, guest parts — learning the craft one set at a time. No overnight fame; just a long apprenticeship.

Then came 2022. As Enid Sinclair, the rainbow-haired werewolf roommate in Tim Burton's Wednesday, Emma became the show's instant fan favorite — the light to Wednesday Addams' shadow. Overnight, a decade of steady work turned into a global fanbase.

Off screen, Emma is known for being refreshingly herself: a devoted K-pop fan (SEVENTEEN especially), a reader, and a famously warm presence at fan events. She has spoken about loving storytelling in every form — and about wanting the characters she plays to feel like real people, not just roles.

The Journey

Milestones, year by year

2010

First steps on set

Emma starts working as a child actress on television in Florida — the very beginning of a fifteen-year (and counting) career.

2017

Holiday movies & growing credits

Teen-era roles in TV movies such as A Taste of Christmas add polish and range while she's still in school.

2021

Indie drama years

Roles in harder-hitting projects like Girl in the Basement show a dramatic instinct far beyond her years.

2022

The world meets Enid Sinclair

Wednesday becomes a global phenomenon and Emma — opposite Jenna Ortega as the colorful werewolf roommate — becomes its breakout heart.

2023

Leading lady on Netflix

Stars as CC Walker in the body-swap comedy Family Switch with Jennifer Garner and Ed Helms, and appears in the indie film Southern Gospel.

2024

Pip Fitz-Amobi takes the case

Emma leads BBC/Netflix's A Good Girl's Guide to Murder, adapting Holly Jackson's bestseller — her first title role, and a hit with book fans and newcomers alike.

2025

Blockbuster spring, gothic fall

As Natalie in A Minecraft Movie she conquers the box office; months later she returns as Enid for Wednesday Season 2, with a worldwide press tour from Seoul to São Paulo.

Next

The story continues

With Wednesday and A Good Girl's Guide to Murder continuing, and new films on the slate, Emma's next chapter is very much still being written.
