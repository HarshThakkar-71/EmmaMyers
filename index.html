The Actress

Colorful on screen,
grounded at heart

Emma Elizabeth Myers was born on April 2, 2002, in Orlando, Florida, and grew up dreaming in movie scenes. Homeschooled and endlessly imaginative, she was acting on television before her teens — more than a decade before the world met her as the sunshine-werewolf Enid Sinclair.

Today she leads Netflix mysteries, tops blockbuster box offices and charms press tours across three continents — all while staying famously herself: a K-pop-loving, book-devouring creative who never forgets a fan.
