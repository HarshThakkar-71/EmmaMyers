Roles that built a career

Werewolves, detectives, big sisters and warriors — every project a new shade of Emma. Filter by screen and explore the filmography.

Signature Role

Wednesday (Netflix, 2022– )

As Enid Sinclair

The werewolf who brought the color

At Nevermore Academy, Emma plays Enid — Wednesday Addams' technicolor werewolf roommate who is still waiting to "wolf out". What began as a supporting role became the soul of the show's biggest friendship, and Emma's international breakout. She returned as a central character for Season 2 in 2025, fronting press tours from London to Seoul.

A Good Girl's Guide to Murder

Pip Fitz-Amobi — lead

As amateur sleuth Pip, Emma carries BBC/Netflix's adaptation of Holly Jackson's bestseller — a five-year-old disappearance, a school project, and a town full of secrets.

2025
Feature Film · Blockbuster

A Minecraft Movie

Natalie

In the live-action trip to the Overworld alongside Jack Black and Jason Momoa, Emma plays Natalie — one of the misfits pulled through a portal into a world made of blocks. A global box-office smash.

2023
Comedy Film

Family Switch

CC Walker

A cosmic mix-up swaps four family members into each other's lives. Emma plays CC — the soccer star daughter — opposite Jennifer Garner and Ed Helms in this feel-good Netflix comedy.

2023
Drama Film

In this period drama about a disgraced preacher seeking redemption, Emma plays Shelia — a small but telling step into serious dramatic territory.
